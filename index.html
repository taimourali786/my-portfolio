Work

Three projects that show how I work: what the problem was, what I did about it, and what changed.

Pricing for 50 million riders

Careem, through Arbisoft

The problem
Careem needed a new pricing engine for its captains and a VAT calculation feature across the MENA region. In pricing, a small bug means lost revenue or angry customers at scale.
What I did
Built the captain pricing engine from scratch (estimates, promotions, trip bonuses, tolls), a shared SDK used by two services so pricing logic lives in one place, and the Kafka pipelines connecting it all. Took on-call for the platform.

VAT pricing shipped on schedule with zero post-launch defects.

80% test coverage on everything I owned, later adopted as the standard by neighbouring teams.

An exchange that stopped going down

Electron X, US electricity trading platform

The problem
A regulated commodity exchange where every environment was set up by hand, outages were noticed late, and traders needed live prices without lag.
What I did
Designed and delivered more than ten microservices on Google Cloud, real-time price and order-book streams over WebSockets, full infrastructure automation with Terraform, and monitoring and structured logging across every service.

Environment provisioning 80% faster.

Production downtime cut by 70%.
